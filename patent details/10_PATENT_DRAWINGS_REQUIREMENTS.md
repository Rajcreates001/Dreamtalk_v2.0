## Drawing Standards

- Black-and-white line art
- Reference numerals consistent across all figures
- Consistent labeling conventions
- Clear data flow arrows
- Component boundaries clearly defined
- No unnecessary detail (support claims only)
- Patent-style formatting (no colors in primary figures)
