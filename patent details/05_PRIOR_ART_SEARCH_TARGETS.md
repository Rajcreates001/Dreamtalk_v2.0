## Important Notes

1. **Read actual claims**, not just titles
2. **Check dependent claims** for specific limitations
3. **Check specification** for technical disclosure
4. **Note filing dates** for prior art timing
5. **Note applicant/assignee** for competitive intelligence
6. **Check citations** in relevant patents for additional prior art
7. **Search GitHub** for open-source implementations of similar concepts
8. **Search arXiv** for academic papers describing similar architectures
