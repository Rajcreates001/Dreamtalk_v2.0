## Ownership Scenarios

### Scenario A: Individual Owner
- Simplest: individual owns all IP
- Can file patent in individual name
- No assignment needed

### Scenario B: Company Owner
- Company owns IP through employment agreements
- Company files patent
- Inventors must assign rights to company

### Scenario C: University/Institution
- University IP office must approve filing
- May require revenue sharing
- May have publication requirements

### Scenario D: Joint Ownership
- Multiple owners must agree on filing
- Complex ownership structure
- May require joint filing

### Scenario E: Government-Funded
- Government may have rights
- Filing may require government approval
- May have mandatory licensing implications
