## If Public Disclosure Has Occurred

### India (Section 31)
- 12-month grace period from date of disclosure
- Filing must occur within 12 months of disclosure
- Disclosure must be by the inventor (not a third party)

### Other Jurisdictions
- Most jurisdictions have NO grace period
- Any public disclosure before filing destroys novelty
- International filing (PCT) must account for earliest disclosure date
