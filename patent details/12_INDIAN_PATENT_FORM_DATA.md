## Abstract (Draft)

A computer-implemented system and method for generating emotion-aware conversational responses in digital twin systems using a neuromorphic spiking neural network brain architecture. The system comprises a spike train encoder that converts text features into spike trains using multiple encoding strategies, five simulated brain areas (prefrontal cortex, dorsal anterior cingulate cortex, insula, inferior parietal lobule, and basal ganglia) interconnected with biologically-inspired learning rules including spike-timing-dependent plasticity and dopamine-modulated eligibility traces, and a basal ganglia action selection module that selects conversational response types through direct and indirect pathway competition. The system further comprises a multi-modal emotion fusion module that dynamically weights voice and text emotion detection based on per-modality confidence scores. The brain area activations modulate a large language model system prompt to produce emotion-aware, action-varied conversational responses synchronized with 3D avatar animation.
